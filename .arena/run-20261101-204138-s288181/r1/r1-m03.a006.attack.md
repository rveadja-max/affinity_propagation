ATTACK 1 [MAJOR] The "chiffre = sortie" check lets through the very numbers that matter most
Where: § 4.9, `grep -nE '[0-9]+[,.][0-9]+|\b[0-9]{2,}\b' Report/chapitres/06_*.qmd`, and the "Fini quand" column of ch. 6 in § 4.2 ("`grep` ne trouve aucun nombre écrit à la main dans ce chapitre").
Problem: The regex only catches decimals and integers of two or more digits. The headline results of this project are single digits: k = 3, 5, 6 or 9 clusters (the a003 table in § 3.2 shows this, and I reproduced it with `apcluster(negDistMat(r=2), scale(iris[,1:4]), q=…, seed=1234)`: 3/5/5/5/6/9/33/149). Counterexample: `echo 'AP produit 9 clusters et 3 exemplaires' | grep -nE '<regex>'` returns nothing, so a hand-typed "9 clusters" passes the check. The check also runs only on ch. 6. The rule it is meant to enforce ("Chaque chiffre du texte doit correspondre à une sortie réellement produite par un script", CLAUDE.md, and in the task: "tout chiffre du rapport doit venir d'un script réellement exécuté") applies to the whole text. Ch. 3.2 (role of the preference), ch. 4 (table of default values, λ 0.9 vs 0.5), ch. 5 (grid, seeds) and ch. 7 (discussion of results) will all contain numbers, and none of them are checked. The plan's main truth safeguard can say "done" when it is not.

ATTACK 2 [MAJOR] The TP shares `00_utils.R` with the report, which brings in the extra packages D5 rules out for the promo
Where: § 4.3, "`analyses/00_utils.R` : … ARI/NMI (D5)" and "Le TP et le rapport partagent `00_utils.R`. Le TP n'a donc pas sa propre version des calculs."; § 4.1 D5, "(a) installer `mclust` … et `aricode` … Le TP ne doit dépendre que d'`apcluster` et de `cluster`, plus `mclust` au maximum".
Problem: D5(a), the recommended option, puts ARI/NMI into `00_utils.R` through `mclust` and `aricode`. If the TP loads that file, students need `aricode` too, which D5 forbids for the TP. Neither package is installed on the reference machine today (`requireNamespace` gives FALSE for both, which I checked). The sharing also makes the TP depend on a relative `source("../analyses/00_utils.R")` (or similar). That call breaks as soon as a student opens `tp/Application_Iris.R` with a different working directory, during a 40-minute session that the cadrage says the promo runs "en temps réel sur leurs machines" (`Affinity Propagation.md:40`). The plan never says how the TP finds `00_utils.R`, and never says which functions it may use without the extra packages. As written, the two rules can't both hold.

ATTACK 3 [MAJOR] Fallback D6(b) breaks the project's rule on the analogy, and the plan's own S8 check contradicts it
Where: § 4.1 D6 (b): "sinon, pas de section : une phrase en conclusion, marquée « analogie conceptuelle »"; § 5 S8: "l'analogie est limitée au ch. 8, et le `grep -n 'ichéité\|idéité'` ne doit trouver que ce fichier".
Problem: The task, quoting CLAUDE.md, says the analogy "est confinée à une section de 500-700 mots", and CLAUDE.md adds "nulle part ailleurs". D6(b) moves it into the conclusion (ch. 9). That is exactly the "ailleurs" the rule forbids, and it produces a short mention instead of a framed 500–700-word section. Under D6(b) there is no ch. 8 file, so the S8 grep either finds the conclusion (check fails) or has nothing to compare against. D6 is the plan's default if the transcript doesn't arrive by 07/11, which is quite likely. The rule-compliant fallback would be to drop the analogy entirely.

ATTACK 4 [MINOR] The forbidden-wording check doesn't look at the right files
Where: § 4.9: `grep -niE 'optimal|…' Report/**/*.qmd`.
Problem: In bash `globstar` is off by default (`shopt globstar` → off). `Report/**/*.qmd` then behaves like `Report/*/*.qmd`. Under D4(b) it skips the main `Report/Affinity_propagation_rapport.qmd`. Under D4(a), the fallback where everything stays in one file, it matches nothing: today `echo Report/**/*.qmd` prints the literal pattern. grep then fails on a missing file, and the output does not show a clean pass or a list of hits. The check for "optimal", "centroïde" and "préférences amorties", which enforces the non-negotiable scientific rules, can be skipped without anyone noticing. Fix: `grep -rniE '…' --include='*.qmd' Report/`.
