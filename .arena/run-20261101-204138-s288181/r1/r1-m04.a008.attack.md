ATTACK 2 [MAJOR] The pam comparison leaves the dissimilarity unspecified, and the sign of the "pam beats AP" result depends on it
Where: §5.2 §4 "Comparateurs à k imposé par AP : `pam`, `kmeans(nstart = 25)`, Ward ; graines nommées" ; §3.2 "pam atteint une somme des distances au carré de 146,68 contre 147,62 pour AP" ; §6 risk "`pam` meilleur qu'AP | plausible (exploration)".
Problem: `methodologie.md:66` and ADR 0003 require "la même dissimilarité (distance euclidienne au carré)" for all methods. The plan's own protocol text drops this, and so do script `03_comparaison_k_egal.R` and the §5.3 instructions. Reproduced on scale(iris[,1:4]) with seed=1234: AP with q=0 gives 147.62. `pam(as.dist(D²), 3, diss=TRUE)` gives 146.68, which matches the plan's figure. Plain `pam(X, 3)` (Euclidean, the default) gives 148.55, which is worse than AP. A student who follows the plan as written runs default `pam` and reaches the opposite conclusion about Brusco & Köhn. The Ward variant (`ward.D` on squared distances vs `ward.D2`) is not specified either. The plan's "comparaison équitable" rule (E9) therefore cannot be carried out without guessing.

ATTACK 3 [MAJOR] The "Fini quand" checks for §5 and §7 give wrong results by design
Where: §5.2 §5 "Fini quand : `grep -E '[0-9]+[,.][0-9]+'` sur le `.qmd` ne trouve aucun chiffre de résultat écrit à la main" ; §7 "aucune occurrence de ces termes ailleurs dans le rapport (`grep -n 'ichéité\|idéité'`)".
Problem: (a) The §5 grep flags things that are allowed and misses things that are forbidden. The plan itself requires parameters in the text ("q de 0 à 1 par pas de 0,05", "λ ∈ {0,5 ; 0,7 ; 0,9}", "seed et paramètres sont dans le texte"), and each of these matches the regex. Meanwhile, integer results typed by hand, such as "9 clusters", "210 itérations", "k = 3" or "setosa 50/0/0", do not match. These integers are exactly the numbers this plan reports from its own exploration. (b) The §7 grep cannot pass. The plan asks the introduction to announce the plan "à mettre à jour", and §7 is titled "…exemplaires, ichéité et idéité", so the announcement repeats the term. The source line itself matches as well (`grep` sees both the `#` heading and the body). Both checks are the plan's stated definition of done, so the user cannot tell whether those sections are finished.
