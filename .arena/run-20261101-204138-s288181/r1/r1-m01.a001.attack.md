ATTACK 6 [MINOR] The `author` constraint is overstated
Where: §3.5 "`author` est une **seule chaîne** séparée par une virgule : le template interdit `\and` (template l. 131-134)".
Problem: `template.tex` l. 134 is `\author{$for(author)$$author$$sep$, $endfor$}`. A YAML list of authors is therefore already joined with ", ". The `\and` ban concerns raw LaTeX, not a YAML list. The rule as written is not needed, and following it means changing Marie's `&` for no reason, which goes against "ne pas réécrire son travail sans accord". The consequence is harmless but the justification is wrong.
