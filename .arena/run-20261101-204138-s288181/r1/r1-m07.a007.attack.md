ATTACK 1 [MAJOR] Stability (and the standardisation test) is made optional, against a stated non-negotiable rule and against the plan's own research question
Where: §4 T5 "Q5 optionnelle … La stabilité (Q5) reste en limite ou en perspective si le temps manque"; §6 R4 "Stabilité (Q5, optionnel) … seulement si R1–R3 sont finis"; §9 checklist (no stability item); §1 Introduction "Ajouter : une question directrice unique, celle du README"; §9 "Conclusion : réponse à la question directrice".
Problem: The task states, among the scientific rules the plan must enforce: "inclure sensibilité, stabilité et résultats négatifs" (CLAUDE.md:81 "Tester la sensibilité (s(k,k), λ) et la stabilité"). The plan makes stability droppable and leaves it out of its own "terminé" checklist, so a report finished by that checklist can omit it. It also contradicts itself: it adopts verbatim the README question (README.md:26-29), which asks how similarity and preference affect "le nombre, la **stabilité** et l'interprétabilité des clusters", then requires the conclusion to answer that question. If R4 is dropped (which the schedule makes likely, see Attack 3), the conclusion cannot answer it. R4 also drops the "avec et sans standardisation" half of Q5 (methodologie.md, §4 table, row Q5), so the plan never tests the standardisation it uses everywhere, although CLAUDE.md asks to justify transformations. Nothing in R1–R3 covers either point.

ATTACK 3 [MAJOR] The pre-oral schedule is overloaded on Hervé and has no fallback, although the plan's only "marge" is after the oral
Where: §7 rows 02/11 and 03/11; "Marge : si le 06/11 dérape, on supprime Wine".
Problem: On Mon 02/11, Hervé is assigned §3 (3.5 pages, six sub-parts, equations copied from the PDF, `verify-claims`, cross-check by Marie), plus `analyses/01` on Iris, plus the theory slides. On Tue 03/11, the plan schedules §4, §5 (Iris), `analyses/02`, the fiche that must be sent that day, and "TP complété", with Hervé named on §4 and §5. A 3.5-page mathematical section plus a script plus slides in one day is the riskiest point of the plan, and it has no fallback (for example: §3.5–3.6 moved after the oral, R1 limited to the `q` grid). The plan's own §3 says "Le vrai risque … ce sont les échéances du 03, 04 et 05/11", but its contingency covers only the period after them.
