ATTACK 1 [MAJOR] The core K-medoids comparison (r3) leaves the dissimilarity open, so the p-median sums it reports measure different objectives
Where: §1 row 6 "R3 : AP face à `pam`, K-means et Ward … avec somme des dissimilarités" ; §3.1 chunk `r3` "AP / `pam` / `kmeans(nstart = 25)` / Ward" ; §1 row 4 "`cluster::pam`, `hclust(ward.D2)`"
Problem: AP runs on `negDistMat(r = 2)`, the negative squared Euclidean distance. `cluster::pam(x, k)` on the standardised matrix uses the plain Euclidean distance by default, so it minimises Σ‖x−m‖ and not Σ‖x−m‖². The plan never says to pass `pam` a squared-distance `dist` object (`pam(as.dist(-s), k, diss = TRUE)` or `dist(x)^2`). `methodologie.md:65` requires "la même dissimilarité (distance euclidienne au carré)" for the comparison, and CLAUDE.md asks for a fair comparison. If the user follows the plan literally, the "somme des dissimilarités" column puts AP's squared-error objective next to pam's unsquared one. The Brusco & Köhn test that the plan relies on (§1 row 7) then means nothing, even though the plan says this table is never cut (§3.4). The "fini quand" criterion for section 5 ("`review-r` … aucune étiquette") does not catch this either.

ATTACK 3 [MAJOR] The stability chunk r5 cannot be built without guessing
Where: §3.1 chunk table, `r5` "50 sous-échantillons à 80 %, ARI moyen et écart-type, AP contre K-means"
Problem: The plan does not say what the ARI is computed between: two subsamples on their shared points (`methodologie.md:47`), or each subsample against the full-data partition. It does not say which preference AP uses on each subsample: the median is recomputed per subsample, so k can change from one draw to the next. It does not say which k K-means gets: the k from AP on that draw, or a fixed k (and a fixed k=3 would leak the labels). Each choice gives different numbers and a different reading. The plan also drops the "avec et sans standardisation" arm of Q5 (`methodologie.md:73`) without saying so, and reduces B from 100 to 50. Stability is a non-negotiable rule in CLAUDE.md, yet section 6.5 has no "fini quand" criterion beyond "chiffres en ligne".
